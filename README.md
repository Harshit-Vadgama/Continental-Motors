# 🏎️ Continental Motors — Luxury Supercar Portal

> **Beyond Luxury. Beyond Limits.**

Continental Motors is an ultra-premium luxury supercar & hypercar digital portal featuring an interactive showroom, priority vehicle reservation system, client receipt generator, bespoke legal policies, and a real-time Executive Admin Dashboard.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🏎️ Luxury Showroom & Experience
- **Epic Trilogy & Brand Showcase**: Full interactive listings for top luxury brands (Aston Martin, Audi, Bentley, BMW, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Maserati, Mercedes-Benz, Mercedes-AMG, Pagani, Porsche, and Rolls-Royce).
- **Video Previews & Specs**: Dynamic hover video playback, vehicle specifications (HP, Top Speed, Engine, Price in ₹), and details toggle.

### 🔐 Account Authentication & Role Management
- **Tabbed Log In / Sign Up Modal**: Full authentication flow supporting Client and Executive Admin account creation.
- **Session Persistence**: User sessions stored via LocalStorage with dynamic navbar profile display (`👤 [User Name]`).
- **Passcode Protection**: Admin account creation protected with security passcode (`admin123`).

### ⚡ Executive Admin Dashboard (`admin.html`)
- **Real-Time Revenue Analytics**: Live tracking of total revenue value (₹) and total vehicle reservations.
- **Reservation Tracking**: Instant order synchronization displaying client names, emails, car model, date, and booking status.
- **User Account Oversight**: Comprehensive list of registered clients and active admins.

### 🧾 Client Receipt & Invoice System (`receipt.html`)
- **Instant Booking Receipt**: Automatically generates structured client reservation invoices upon booking confirmation.
- **Downloadable Receipt File**: One-click download of `.txt` receipt vouchers for offline record keeping.

### 📑 Customer Support & Legal Governance
- **Interactive FAQ Accordion**: Expandable Q&A covering booking steps, viewing suites, and white-glove delivery logistics.
- **Terms of Service & Privacy Policy**: Bespoke legal disclosures tailored for high-value automobile transactions.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser to view the showroom.
2. Register an account using the `👤 Account` button.
3. Access the Executive Admin Panel by creating an Executive Admin account using passcode `admin123` or opening `admin.html`.
