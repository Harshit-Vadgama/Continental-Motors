Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are paramount to Continental Motors.

1. Data We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when creating an account or reserving a vehicle. This includes your full name, email address, phone number, reservation history, and vehicle preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilized to process vehicle reservation receipts, coordinate white-glove delivery, verify identity for high-value transactions, respond to concierge inquiries, and send tailored luxury vehicle announcements if requested.

3. Data Protection & Confidentiality

Continental Motors employs enterprise-grade encryption protocols and strict access controls to safeguard client credentials and financial invoice records. We never sell, rent, or trade client personal data to third-party marketers.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Our platform uses local browser storage to temporarily save active reservation receipts and user preferences (such as profile details) to ensure a fluid browsing experience across sessions.
